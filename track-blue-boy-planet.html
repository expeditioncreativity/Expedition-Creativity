The Story

Blue Boy Planet is inspired by the story of a 6-year-old boy whose journey continues far beyond our world. Through music and visuals, this short film reimagines loss as a cosmic voyage: a young boy decides to leave Earth behind to explore distant planets, galaxies and the mysteries of the universe.
