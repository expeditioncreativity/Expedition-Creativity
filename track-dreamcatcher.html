The Story

Dreamcatcher is an early orchestral soundtrack written for a 40+ piece orchestra. It explores a frontier atmosphere with tribal colours, broad cinematic gestures and a sense of mystery.

The piece was created in Ableton Live 10 Standard, with sound effects obtained from Zapsplat. The mix status for this track is intentionally noted as unmixed, preserving the raw sketch-like energy of the composition.
