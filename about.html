About Expedition Creativity

Behind every expedition is a storyteller.

Expedition Creativity is the personal creative hub of Mikaël Stroobants: cinematic music, orchestral storytelling and imaginative journeys inspired by nature, adventure and film music.

About me

My musical journey began with the French horn, an instrument that taught me how melody, breath and colour can shape emotion.

For 15 years, I played in a local band. That experience taught me discipline, ensemble playing, live performance and the joy of creating something bigger together.

At the same time, my passion for film music kept growing. Composers like John Williams, Hans Zimmer, James Newton Howard, Harry Gregson-Williams and Howard Shore showed me how music can build entire worlds.

Expedition Creativity brings those influences together. Every track starts from a scene, a feeling or a question, and grows into a small cinematic expedition.

“Every track is more than a composition. Every track is an expedition.”

Who is Expedition Creativity for?

Whether you are a conductor looking for original concert band repertoire, a filmmaker searching for music for film or documentaries, a musician seeking new orchestral works, or simply a lover of cinematic music, you are welcome to explore.

Expedition Creativity brings together original compositions that combine orchestral writing with musical storytelling, inviting every listener on a new creative expedition.
