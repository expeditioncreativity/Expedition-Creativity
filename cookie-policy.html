Privacy & Cookies

Cookie Policy

Expedition Creativity uses essential website functionality and optional analytics cookies to understand how visitors discover and use the website.

Essential cookies

Essential cookies are used to remember your cookie preference and keep the website functioning as intended. These cannot be disabled through the website because they are necessary for basic operation.

Analytics cookies

Analytics cookies are only enabled after you choose Accept analytics. They are used through Google Analytics 4 to measure anonymous website visits, page views and general usage patterns.

No advertising cookies are used, and Expedition Creativity does not sell visitor data.

Change your preference

You can reopen the cookie banner at any time using the Cookie preferences button in the footer.
